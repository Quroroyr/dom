01/ 07House

A house made of weather. Press it, pull pieces away, look inside.

Soft to the touch.

Pinch anywhere and pull. The piece tears free and floats; the house keeps the hollow.

Inside, the
light is kept.

The front layer lifts like an awning. Drag to look around.

A house
that drifts.

Lift one layer, or let every part float. Reassemble brings it home.

Cloud, cream,
sugar, mist.

Tap the house to change what it is made of.

Colour arrives
like weather.

Pick a sky: the light and the air change with it.

Pigment,
soaked in.

Paint the house, or any piece you pulled away. Each piece keeps its own colour.

This house is drawn live, and this browser can’t draw it.
